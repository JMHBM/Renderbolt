# Welcome to Renderbolt

You made it. This is the workshop.

Renderbolt turns a song and a still into a cinematic MP4 **on your machine**. No upload. No account. No cloud queue chewing on your track. Version **1.0.7** is out as a Debian / Ubuntu `.deb` — that is the build we have installed, used, and will stand behind.

Everything else is coming: Fedora once it survives JMHBM’s Silverblue box, then other Linux, then Windows, then macOS. Same studio. Native packages. Nothing ships until someone here has actually run it.
